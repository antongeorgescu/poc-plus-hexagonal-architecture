STUDENT LOAN PORTAL POC - SAMPLE QUESTIONS

Tags: [B] is used for neutral, transactional phrasing and [C] for questions with emotional or situational context (hardship, worry, urgency). This is an assumption; adjust it if the tags mean something else.

User types on the slide
  Type 1 - Excellent standing (alice, grace): positive sentiment, rewards/refinance offers, upbeat theme.
  Type 2 - Delinquency grade 1 (bob, henry): somewhat negative sentiment, autopay/budgeting help, encouraging theme.
  Type 3 - Delinquency grade 2 (carol, irene): very negative sentiment, hardship assistance/debt counseling, supportive theme.

 2. [B] I'd like to know what's the balance of my loan and when's the maturity date?
 3. [B] I'd like to make an extra payment to my loan balance.
 4. [B] What's my current balance and payoff amount?

 9. [C] How much extra should I pay off my loan in 12 months?
10. [C] I may not be able to make a few payments for the time being as I lost my job
11. [C] I might miss a payment this month - what are my options?
12. [C] How do I set up autopay for my monthly payments
13. [C] I just got my first child and I'd like to know if I qualify for an interest rate relief.
14. [C] Due to unforeseen circumstances, I can't afford my payments right now. What repayment assistance is available to me?
15. [C] I'm struggling to pay and may miss payments - what help is available?
16. [B] I'd like to pay down my principal by an extra $150 this month.
17. [C] What do you recommend to deal with hardship and inability to make payments?
